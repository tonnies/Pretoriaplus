About the data
  • Results: IEC detailed results for the 2016 and 2021 local government elections. 2026 results appear once loaded with pipeline/ingest_2026.py.
  • Votes are from the party-list (PR) ballot, which offers the same parties in every ward. The ward detail on the map also shows the ward-candidate ballot.
  • Groups use the FF+ share of the PR vote in 2021: Stronghold 15% or more, Solid 5–15%, Marginal 1–5%, Negligible under 1%.
  • "Estimated on 2026 wards": the 2026 wards were redrawn, so 2021 voting-district results are split across them by area.
  • Area names: suburbs from Stats SA Census 2011, ranked by the number of voters living in each.
