About the data
  • Results: IEC detailed results files for the 2016 and 2021 local government elections, per voting district. 2026 results appear here once loaded with pipeline/ingest_2026.py.
  • Boundaries: Municipal Demarcation Board wards (2016, 2020 and 2026 sets) and IEC voting districts.
  • "On 2026 wards" views are estimates: older voting-district results are split across the redrawn 2026 wards by area.
  • Support tiers use the PR list share: Stronghold ≥ 15%, Solid 5–15%, Marginal 1–5%, Negligible under 1%.
  • A party with no ward candidate gets zero ward-ballot votes. FF+ stood in all 107 wards in 2021 and in 105 in 2016 (not in wards 12 and 21).
